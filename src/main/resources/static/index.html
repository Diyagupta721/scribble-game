Quick rules

How to Play

  1. Take turns as the Artist. Each round, one player gets a secret word and draws it.
  2. Everyone else guesses in the chat panel as fast as they can.
  3. Faster correct guesses score more. The artist earns points too when others guess right.
  4. Rounds rotate until everyone has been the artist for the chosen number of rounds.
  5. Highest total score wins when the game ends.
